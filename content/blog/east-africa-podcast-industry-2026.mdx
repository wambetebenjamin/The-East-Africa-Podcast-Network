Every quarter we publish the network's numbers and what they tell us about the industry. Here is the October 2026 edition.

## The headline numbers

Across our ten shows: 250,000 monthly listeners, 1.1 million monthly streams, and — the number advertisers care about — an average completion rate of 68% on mid-rolls. Podcast advertising works in East Africa not because the audience is huge, but because it stays.

## Ad rates are up, CPMs are converging

Mid-roll rates on premium East African shows now clear KES 25,000–60,000 per episode for 30,000+ listen shows. Host-read creative commands a 20–30% premium over produced spots, consistent with global patterns.

## Video podcasts are not eating audio

YouTube versions of podcast episodes grow discovery but do not cannibalise audio streams — our data shows listeners who find a show on YouTube migrate to audio within a month. Publish both.

## What we are watching

1. **Swahili-first originals** — under-served and growing fastest.
2. **Programmatic audio** — the plumbing is finally arriving in the region.
3. **Cross-border co-productions** — Kenyan, Tanzanian and Ugandan studios sharing costs and audiences.

## The honest caveats

Measurement remains the industry's weak point. Third-party verification is thin, and downloads ≠ listens. At EAPN we report listener-verified streams and encourage every buyer to ask for the methodology behind any number they are quoted — including ours.
