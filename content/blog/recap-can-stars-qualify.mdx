When we planned the fixture-by-fixture episode, we expected arguments. We did not expect four thousand voice notes in three days. This is a recap of the episode — and of your responses.

## The episode, briefly

We broke down all six fixtures with analyst Tom Olunga: the Casablanca away day we all fear, the homecoming at Kasarani, and the November decider. Tom's verdict: Stars need four points from the first three matches or the maths turns ugly.

## What you told us

- **61%** of you believe Stars qualify. Optimism we did not expect.
- The **most-cited key player** was not the striker everyone assumed — it was the defensive midfielder.
- Dozens of you sent tactical diagrams. Two were better than ours. You know yourselves.

## The correction

We said the 2004 final was in Tunis. Several of you correctly noted the venue was Rades. We stand corrected, on air, next episode. This is why we do the show.
